Scope

Mini, major and IEEE projects, across branches

Most of what we build is software: web applications, dashboards, REST APIs, machine-learning models wrapped in an interface somebody can actually use, and IoT projects where a device reports into a web backend. That covers the bulk of CSE, IT, ISE, AI/ML, data science, MCA, BCA and MSc requirements, and the software half of ECE, EEE and mechatronics projects where hardware feeds a dashboard.

If your guide has already fixed the domain, or handed you an IEEE paper, send it across. We will read it and tell you plainly which parts are reproducible in the time you have and which depend on a dataset or a piece of hardware you are unlikely to get hold of. If you have no idea yet, say that instead — it is a completely normal starting point, and we will come back with two or three options that suit your branch, your skill level and your submission date rather than whatever is quickest for us to build.

One thing we won't do

We won't pretend you built something you have never looked at. The walkthrough and the commented code exist precisely so you can stand in front of an examiner and account for every decision in the project. If you want to write part of it yourself — a module, the report, the front end — tell us which part, and we will leave it to you and review it instead of replacing it.
